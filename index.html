About Dr. Bishal Mani Poudel

Dr. Bishal Mani Poudel is a psychiatrist with professional interests in general adult psychiatry, psychotherapy, neuropsychiatry and psychosomatic medicine.

His approach emphasizes understanding each individual's experiences, providing appropriate assessment and developing personalized treatment plans through compassionate and evidence-informed care.

Areas of Clinical Interest

Clinical Services

Assessment and management of mental health concerns across different stages of adult life.

General Adult Psychiatry

Assessment and management of common and complex adult mental health conditions.

Psychotherapy

Psychological therapies and supportive approaches to emotional and behavioural difficulties.

Neuropsychiatry

Clinical care involving the relationship between neurological conditions and mental health.

Psychosomatic Medicine

Understanding and addressing the interaction between psychological wellbeing and physical symptoms.
